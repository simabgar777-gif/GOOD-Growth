Full description (≤2000)

GOOD Growth is a workshop for growth.

We do one thing: turn a goal into a review, a rhythm and ready posts. You write what
you want in one sentence. We show who to speak to and about what, how often, and we
build the texts and images. No one-size-fits-all templates: every brand has its own
memo — voice, facts, stop-words, audience. That is why posts sound like you, not like
advertising.

Growth is not luck. It is craft. We do not promise to "blow up in a week" and we do not
feed you numbers for pretty reports. Five steps, a calm rhythm, an honest result:

  1. Goal — one sentence about your work.
  2. Review — who to speak to and about what.
  3. Rhythm — how many times a week.
  4. Posts — texts and images.
  5. Feedback — what worked.

What matters: we do not write "user", we do not send manipulative notifications and we
do not sell data. Your texts stay yours.

Start with one goal. The first review is free.

7. Voice — allowed and forbidden

Allowed:

· short sentences, real details, numbers from the work
· honesty about limits: "not yet", "we are building it"
· silence instead of a call to action (not every post sells)

Forbidden:

· "user" — say "person", "you"
· "guaranteed result", "blow up in a week", "cheap"
· artificial urgency: "only 2 spots left", countdowns
· emoji fireworks in every paragraph, caps, exclamation marks
· promoting other services or promising on a client's behalf

_If in doubt — ask. One honest post beats five beautiful posts about nothing._
